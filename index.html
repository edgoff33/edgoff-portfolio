I turn ambiguous, high-consequence technology risk into a capability the organization can operate long after I have moved on.

That starts before any proposal. I invest early in learning the business - its processes, its people, its strategy, and where the real challenges and opportunities sit.

A security strategy earns its funding when its objectives, goals, tactics, and measures are expressed in the terms the business already uses to make decisions. Aligned that way, security and technology enable growth and company objectives rather than tax them.
